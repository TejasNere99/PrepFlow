# PrepFlow Gamification & Student Engagement System

# Vision

PrepFlow is not just a study platform.

It is a platform that motivates students to study consistently by rewarding progress, celebrating achievements, and making learning enjoyable.

Every gamification feature should support learning—not distract from it.

The goal is to transform studying from a boring routine into an engaging experience while maintaining educational value.

---

# Core Philosophy

> **Study consistently.
> Build habits.
> Celebrate progress.
> Enjoy learning.**

Students should feel excited every time they open PrepFlow.

---

# Engagement Pillars

The engagement system is built on six pillars.

* Daily Consistency
* Healthy Competition
* Rewards
* Recognition
* Community
* Positive Reinforcement

# Prep Coins

Prep Coins are the official in-platform reward currency.

Students earn coins through meaningful learning activities.

Coins are never purchased through learning activities—they are earned.

# PrepTV – Personalized Motivation Engine

## Vision

PrepTV is a personalized motivational panel displayed on every student's profile.

Instead of showing static banners, PrepTV displays short motivational animations, humorous clips, and positive reactions based on the student's learning activity.

Its purpose is to make the platform feel alive and emotionally engaging.

---

# How PrepTV Works

PrepTV analyzes student activity.

Examples

* Daily Streak
* Current Streak
* Recent Performance
* Contest Results
* Battle Results
* Achievements
* Level Ups
* Chapter Completion
* Inactivity

Based on this activity, PrepTV selects an appropriate motivational animation.

---

# Original Content Policy

PrepTV should use only original assets.

Do not use copyrighted memes, movie clips, anime scenes, or copyrighted GIFs.

Instead, PrepFlow should develop its own mascot, illustrations, animations, and humorous content.

This ensures:

* Brand identity
* Copyright safety
* Long-term scalability
