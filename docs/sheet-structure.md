# PrepFlow Chapter Sheet Structure

# Vision

PrepFlow is not just a collection of educational resources.

It is a **structured learning roadmap** that guides students from the beginning of a chapter to complete mastery.

Instead of asking:

> "What should I study next?"

PrepFlow answers:

> "This is your next step."

Every chapter follows the same structured learning flow to eliminate confusion and provide a consistent study experience.

---

# Learning Philosophy

Every chapter is divided into structured learning stages.

Each stage has a clear objective.

Students complete one stage before moving to the next.

This creates a predictable and distraction-free learning experience.

---

# Learning Flow

Students always follow the same order.

```text
Lectures
      │
      ▼
Notes
      │
      ▼
Solved Questions / Practice
      │
      ▼
Exercise
      │
      ▼
PYQs
      │
      ▼
Chapter Challenge
      │
      ▼
One Shot Revision
```

This removes decision fatigue.

Students always know the next step.

# Lecture Page

## Teacher Selection

Instead of dropdowns, PrepFlow uses horizontal tabs.

Example

```text
[Dinesh Sir] [PW] [GanitAnk] [RG Lectures]
```

The first teacher is selected by default.

Clicking another teacher instantly switches the playlist without reloading the page.

---

# Completion Philosophy

A chapter is considered completed only when all required learning stages are finished.

Merely watching lectures does not complete a chapter.

Completion represents meaningful preparation.

---

# Design Principles

The entire sheet structure follows these principles.

* One learning objective per page.
* Consistent UI across all chapters.
* Horizontal tabs for switching resources.
* No unnecessary complexity.
* Progress visible everywhere.
* Students always know the next step.
* Distraction-free interface.
* Mobile-first navigation.
